# Code of conduct

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where people work on
`agent-elapsed`.

## Expected behaviour

* **Argue about the work, not the person.** "This coverage gate will reject a fair model at n = 20,
  here is the arithmetic" is good. "You clearly don't understand statistics" is not.
* **Show your evidence.** Claims about correctness, accuracy or performance are welcome when they come
  with a reproduction, a derivation or a measurement. This project has a documented habit of writing
  down what is *not* proven; contribute in that spirit.
* **Assume the other person is trying to make the number honest.** Disagreements here are usually about
  which limit matters more, not about bad faith.
* **Be patient with non-native speakers.** Issues and translations arrive in several languages, and
  imperfect grammar is not a reason to dismiss a report.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission — including real task logs, hostnames or
  task identifiers taken from someone else's telemetry.
* Dismissing a reproduction with "it works on my machine" and no further investigation.
* Sending a security report to a public issue instead of the private advisory flow in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through the repository's private channels (a security advisory or a
direct message to the maintainer). Reports are handled confidentially. Responses scale with the
problem: a clarification, a comment removal, a temporary block, or a permanent ban from the
repository. Maintainers who violate this document are held to it as well.
